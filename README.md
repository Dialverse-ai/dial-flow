<h1 align="center">Yalla Flow</h1>
<p align="center"><b>Arabic + English dictation for Windows — by Dialverse</b><br>
Press F9 anywhere, speak Arabic, English, or both — your words appear where your cursor is.</p>

---

## Download / التحميل

**[⬇ Download Yalla Flow (latest)](../../releases/latest)** — grab `YallaFlow.exe`, that's the whole app.

## Setup — 3 minutes / التثبيت

1. **Run `YallaFlow.exe`.** Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first
   time — click **More info → Run anyway**. This is normal for internal tools.
   <br>أول مرة ويندوز ممكن يحذرك — دوس **More info** بعدين **Run anyway**. ده طبيعي.
2. **Get your free key:** sign up at [dashboard.cohere.com](https://dashboard.cohere.com/api-keys),
   copy the **Trial key**, and paste it into the app's welcome screen.
   <br>اعمل حساب مجاني وانسخ الـ Trial key والصقه في شاشة الترحيب.
3. **Dictate:** press **F9** in any app (WhatsApp, Outlook, Word…), speak, press **F9**
   again — the text pastes at your cursor. **F10** switches Arabic/English.
   <br>دوس **F9** في أي برنامج واتكلم عربي أو انجليزي أو الاتنين مع بعض.

## Tips

- **Flow mode** (on the Dictation page) cleans filler words and punctuation with AI.
- Closing the window keeps Yalla Flow running in the **system tray** — hotkeys stay live.
- Turn on **Settings → Start with Windows** so it's always ready.
- Add client names to the **Dictionary** so they're always spelled right.

## Requirements

Windows 10/11. Each person uses their own free Cohere key — your audio and history
stay on your machine.
